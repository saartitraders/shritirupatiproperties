Website privacy

This page explains how this website's enquiry form and external links work.

Your enquiry

The form prepares a WhatsApp message using the details you enter. When you select “Continue to WhatsApp”, those details are included in a link to WhatsApp so that it can prepare your draft. You decide whether to send the message. The website itself does not save form entries to a database or browser storage.

External services

WhatsApp, Google Maps, your email application and your telephone application handle any interactions after you open their links, under their own policies. Google Fonts supplies the website's fonts and receives the connection information needed to serve them. The hosting provider may process standard request information to deliver and protect the site.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts or its own cookies.
